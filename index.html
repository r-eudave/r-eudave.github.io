01 / About

Between places,
people, and data.

My work connects heritage conservation with the possibilities of digital technologies, without losing sight of the people who give places their meaning.

With a background in architecture and civil engineering, I have worked across Mexico, Portugal, Italy, Switzerland, and the Netherlands. My experience spans post-earthquake restoration and seismic vulnerability assessment, photogrammetry, ontologies, and community-led heritage documentation.

At TU Delft, I am developing CORDHISK: a Memory Twin approach that connects community-contributed memories with interoperable metadata to document heritage at risk.
