Curriculum vitae · October 2026

Rafael Ramírez Eudave, PhD

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow in Digital Heritage and Heritage at Risk

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at TU Delft working on digital heritage, heritage at risk, and machine learning-supported seismic vulnerability assessment. Over seven years of research across Mexico, Portugal, Italy, Switzerland, and the Netherlands, combining field damage surveys and materials testing with GIS, ontologies, and open-source software. Author of 21 journal articles (14 as first author), 6 book chapters, and 4 open datasets and software releases; secured over €260k in competitive research funding.
